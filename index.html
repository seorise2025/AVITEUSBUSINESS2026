Questions

What firms ask before they switch

Still unsure? Call 020 4628 8209.

How much will it cost?

You pay one fixed monthly fee, agreed in writing before we start. It covers the services in your plan. If your needs change, we agree a new fee with you first.

Is it hard to leave my current accountant?

No. With your permission we contact them, ask for your records and handle the handover. Most switches need nothing from you beyond a signature.

Do I need to use Xero, Sage or QuickBooks?

We work in all three every day. If you are on spreadsheets or paper, we help you move and train your team.

Do you only work with firms in London?

No. Our office is in Harrow, and we serve SMEs and startups across the UK by phone, video and secure cloud tools.

Who will I speak to?

A named manager who knows your business. You will not be passed around a call centre.

# DeadlineTag

A date block with a short label for HMRC deadlines and key dates.

Intentional addition: tax dates are the strongest reason UK firms contact an accountant, and the content plan leans on them.

**Consumer provides:** day, month and year, a title (max 3 words) and a form or period line.

- The date sits on `night` with the day in `ember-glow`. Text uses the `code` style.
- Use in blog posts, the services page and email headers.
- Check every date against GOV.UK before publishing.

**Do not** show a deadline that has passed, or more than 3 tags in one block.

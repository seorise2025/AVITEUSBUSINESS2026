# CTABand

A full-width `night` band with one question and one ember button, cut at the top-right with `cut-l`.

**Consumer provides:** eyebrow, a question headline (max 10 words), one button.

- Place it once in the middle of long pages and once before the footer.
- Write the headline as a question the reader answers "yes" to.

**Do not** use a second button, or reuse copy from the hero.

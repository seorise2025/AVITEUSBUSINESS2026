# Logo

The Aviteus Business logo, used exactly as supplied: rising bars, the pulse line and the wordmark "Accuracy. Integrity. Results".

**Consumer provides:** a link target and the width (min 120px, default 200px in headers, 220px in footers).

- On white or `ground` (Daylight): place the PNG directly with `.av-logo`.
- On `night`, `night-deep` or anywhere in Night Ledger: wrap it in `.av-logo--plate`. The plate is `plate` white with `radius-m` and `shadow-plate`. The wordmark ink (`logo-ink`) and slate bars disappear on navy without it.
- Clear space: keep at least `space-4` on every side.

**Do not** recolour, stretch, crop, add effects, rebuild the mark in code, or place it on `ember`. Alt text: "Aviteus Business".

# Hero

The opening screen: a `night` band with the promise on the left, a short quote form on the right, and the wide Pulse Line drawing across the base.

**Consumer provides:** eyebrow, headline (max 9 words) with one accent word, lead (max 30 words), two buttons, up to 3 badges, a 2 to 3 field form.

- Headline uses `display-xl`; the accent word is `ember-glow` via `.av-accent`.
- The form sits on `night-raised` with light inputs. It asks for name and email only.
- The Pulse Line draws once on load. No video, no carousel, no stock photo behind the text.
- Below 820px the form stacks under the text and the headline drops to 48px.

**Do not** put a photo behind the headline. The site's current photo hero lowers contrast and slows the page.

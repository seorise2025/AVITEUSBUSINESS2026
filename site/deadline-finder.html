How these dates are worked out

These are the standard rules for most UK limited companies and their directors.

WhatDeadlineExample
VAT return and payment1 month and 7 days after the end of each VAT periodQuarter ending 30 November: due 7 January
Annual accounts to Companies House9 months after your year endYear end 31 March: due 31 December
Corporation tax payment9 months and 1 day after your year endYear end 31 March: due 1 January
Company tax return (CT600)12 months after your year endYear end 31 March: due 31 March the next year
PAYE and National Insurance22nd of each month, paying electronicallyTax month ending 5 June: due 22 June
P60s to employees31 May each yearFor the tax year that ended on 5 April
P11D and P11D(b) forms6 July each yearBenefits in kind for the tax year that ended on 5 April
Self Assessment return and tax31 January after the tax year ends2025/26 tax year: due 31 January 2027

When these rules do not fit

Your first accounts after setting up a company have a different deadline. Large companies pay corporation tax in instalments. If a payment date falls on a weekend or bank holiday, make sure the money reaches HMRC by the working day before. Always check your own dates on GOV.UK or with your accountant.

Deadline questions

When is corporation tax due for a limited company?

For most small companies, 9 months and 1 day after the end of the accounting period. With a year end of 31 March, the payment is due on 1 January. The company tax return (CT600) is due later, 12 months after the year end.

When do I file my company accounts at Companies House?

9 months after your company's year end, for a private limited company after its first accounts. With a year end of 31 March, the deadline is 31 December.

When is PAYE due each month?

If you pay HMRC electronically, PAYE and National Insurance are due by the 22nd of the month, for the tax month that ended on the 5th.

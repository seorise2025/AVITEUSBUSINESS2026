Who we work with

Owner-managed businesses in any sector, anywhere in the UK. If your business sells, employs people or files with HMRC, we can help.

  • Limited companies and their directors. Books, payroll, VAT and year-end accounts, and the directors' own tax returns.
  • Sole traders and the self-employed. Bookkeeping and Self Assessment, with Making Tax Digital for Income Tax in hand.
  • Partnerships and LLPs. Partnership accounts and each partner's tax return.
  • Landlords. Rental income records and Self Assessment.
  • Businesses changing accountant. We handle the handover. Use our free switching accountant checklist to see every step.
  • Startups and new companies. Company formation, registrations and the first year of deadlines.
  • Growing teams of 2 to 50 staff. Monthly management accounts, payroll and, when you need it, a Virtual CFO.

Every sector has its own rules for payroll, VAT and records. We ask about yours on the first call and build your written scope around the way you work. Book a free 20-minute review.

Also available, as part of a monthly service

  • Company set-up: forming a limited company and registering it with HMRC.
  • Software set-up and clean-up: moving you onto Xero, QuickBooks, Sage or FreeAgent, or tidying a file that has got out of hand.
  • Catch-up bookkeeping: bringing old records up to date before monthly work starts.

What we don't do

We do not carry out statutory audits: those need a registered audit firm. If your company needs one, we will point you to a firm and support you through it, as part of our audit support. Saying no to work we are not set up for is how we keep the work we do take on to a high standard.

What we do, and what we don't

Three services, done every month for UK owner-managed businesses. Most clients use two or three together, because they all run off the same set of books.

Bookkeeping and management accounts

For owners whose books are behind, or who want to see profit and cash every month.

You get: reconciled bank accounts, bills and sales posted, a three-page monthly pack.

See how bookkeeping works

Payroll and pensions

For businesses paying 2 to 50 people who want payroll off their desk.

You get: staff paid on the right day, payslips, RTI filed, workplace pensions handled.

See how payroll works

VAT and year-end tax

For VAT-registered companies that want every deadline known and met.

You get: VAT returns under Making Tax Digital, year-end accounts, corporation tax.

See VAT and tax deadlines

Also available, as part of a monthly service

  • Company set-up: forming a limited company and registering it with HMRC.
  • Software set-up and clean-up: moving you onto Xero, QuickBooks, Sage or FreeAgent, or tidying a file that has got out of hand.
  • Catch-up bookkeeping: bringing old records up to date before monthly work starts.

What we don't do

We do not carry out statutory audits, business valuations or fundraising. If you need one of those, we will point you to a firm that does. Saying no to work we are not set up for is how we keep the work we do take on to a high standard.

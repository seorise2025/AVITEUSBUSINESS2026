What business advisory means with Aviteus

Business advisory means using your figures to make decisions, not just to file returns. With Aviteus it starts from up-to-date books, so every forecast rests on real numbers. You get a forecast you can update, a budget to measure against, and a short list of actions that protect cash and support growth.

What is included

  • Cash flow forecasts. What comes in and goes out over the coming months, built from your books and your plans.
  • Budgets. Targets for the year, compared with your actual figures each month.
  • Pricing reviews. The margin on each product or service, so you can see which ones pay their way.
  • Cost controls. Where the money goes: suppliers, subscriptions and the costs that crept up.
  • Funding readiness. The forecasts and figures a bank or lender asks for, prepared before you apply.

Who this is for, and who it isn't

This suits you if you run a growing UK business and want to see the next few months before they happen: before a hire, a big purchase, a price change or a loan.

We do not raise funds, arrange loans or act as a broker. If you need investment raised, we will point you to a firm that does. And if your books are behind, we start with a catch-up, because advice built on old figures is guesswork.

How the fee works

A fixed fee for a one-off piece of work, such as a pricing review or a funding pack. Or a fixed monthly fee if you want your forecast and budget kept up to date alongside your bookkeeping. Agreed in writing before any work starts.

Business advisory questions

Do I need monthly bookkeeping first?

You need up-to-date books. If ours is the team keeping them, the forecast stays current every month. If your books are behind, we catch them up first.

Can you help me get a bank loan?

We prepare the forecasts and figures a lender asks for, so your application is ready. We do not arrange loans or act as a broker.

How often should a forecast be updated?

Monthly suits most growing businesses. Quarterly can work if your figures do not change much.

What is the difference between a forecast and a budget?

A budget is the target you set for the year. A forecast is your best view of what will actually happen, updated as things change.

What accounting software set-up means with Aviteus

The right software turns bookkeeping from a monthly chore into a routine that runs itself. We set it up around how your business works: bank feeds connected, VAT set correctly, invoices ready to send. Your subscription stays in your company's name, and you keep full access at all times.

What is included

  • Choosing the right software. Xero, QuickBooks, Sage or FreeAgent, matched to your size, your sector and how you work.
  • Set-up. Chart of accounts, VAT settings, bank feeds and invoice templates.
  • Moving your records. From spreadsheets or another package, with opening balances checked before anything new is posted.
  • Clean-up. A file that has got out of hand: unmatched bank lines, duplicates and wrong VAT codes put right.
  • Training. For you and your team: the two or three things to do each month, and nothing more.
  • Connected apps. Receipt capture apps such as Hubdoc, so bills and receipts reach your books without paper.

How the fee works

A fixed fee for set-up, moving records or a clean-up, quoted before we start. Training is included with set-up. If you then use our monthly bookkeeping, the software becomes part of that routine.

Accounting software questions

Which accounting software is best for a small business?

It depends on your size, your sector and who will use it. Xero, QuickBooks, Sage and FreeAgent all work for UK businesses. We recommend one after a short review.

Can you move my old records into new software?

Yes. We bring across opening balances and, where needed, past transactions, then check the figures before anything new is posted.

Do I need Making Tax Digital software?

If you are VAT registered, yes. Your VAT returns must be filed through compatible software, and all four packages we use are compatible.

Who owns the software subscription?

You do. The subscription stays in your company's name, and you can remove our access at any time.

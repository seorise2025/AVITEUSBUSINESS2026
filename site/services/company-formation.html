What company formation and compliance cover

Company formation means registering a limited company at Companies House. Compliance means keeping it in good standing after that: the yearly confirmation statement, changes to directors and shareholders, and the records the law asks you to keep. We do both, and put every date in your "Done and due" report.

What is included

  • Limited company set-up. Registering the company at Companies House, with directors, shareholders and the registered office address checked first.
  • HMRC registrations. Corporation tax, and VAT and PAYE when you need them.
  • Companies House filings. The confirmation statement each year, and changes to directors, shareholders and addresses.
  • Company secretarial tasks. Keeping the company's registers and minutes of key decisions up to date.
  • Your first dates. First accounts, first corporation tax payment and first confirmation statement, in one calendar.

Company formation questions

How long does it take to set up a limited company?

Online applications to Companies House are often registered within a day, once the details are ready. Most of the time goes into deciding the details first.

When are my first accounts due?

A new company's first accounts are due 21 months after the date it was set up. After that, accounts are due 9 months after each year end.

Do you file the confirmation statement?

Yes. We check the company's details with you each year and file it within 14 days of the review date.

Do I need to register for VAT straight away?

Only if your VAT-taxable turnover goes over the registration threshold, or you expect it to. Some businesses register early by choice. We go through this when we set up the company.

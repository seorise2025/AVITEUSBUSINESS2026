What a Virtual CFO does

A Virtual CFO is a part-time finance director. With Aviteus, that means a qualified, senior person who reviews your figures with you each month, plans cash and growth, and sits beside you for the big decisions: hiring, pricing, borrowing or raising funds.

What is included

  • Monthly finance review. Your management accounts walked through with you, with the three things that need attention.
  • Financial management. Margins, overheads, working capital and the KPIs that show how the business is doing.
  • Cash flow management. A rolling cash forecast, so you see a shortfall weeks before it arrives.
  • Strategic financial planning. An annual plan and budget, and the numbers behind each growth option.
  • Lender and investor support. Business plans, forecasts and the figures lenders and investors ask for, with support through their questions.

Who this is for, and who it isn't

This suits you if your business has outgrown basic bookkeeping and you want senior finance input every month, without hiring a full-time finance director.

If you only need your books kept and your returns filed, our monthly bookkeeping and VAT services will be enough. We will say so on the call.

How the fee works

A fixed monthly fee, based on how much time you need each month. Agreed in writing before any work starts, and reviewed with you as the business grows.

Virtual CFO questions

What is the difference between a Virtual CFO and an accountant?

An accountant keeps your records and files your returns. A Virtual CFO uses those figures to plan ahead and advise on decisions. Many clients use both, from the same team.

How often will we meet?

Usually once a month, with more contact around big decisions, year end or a funding round.

Can a Virtual CFO help us raise money?

Yes. We prepare the business plan, forecasts and investor or lender pack, and support you through their questions and checks.

Do we need monthly bookkeeping as well?

You need up-to-date books. If ours is the team keeping them, your Virtual CFO works from current figures every month.

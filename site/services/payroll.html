What a managed payroll service includes

A managed payroll service means a person runs your payroll for you, start to finish. With Aviteus that covers calculating pay, tax and National Insurance, sending payslips, reporting each pay run to HMRC through Real Time Information (RTI), and handling workplace pension contributions. You approve the figures before anyone is paid.

What we handle

  • Weekly, fortnightly, four-weekly or monthly pay runs
  • Payslips sent securely to each employee
  • RTI submissions to HMRC for every pay run
  • Starters and leavers, including P45s
  • Statutory pay: sick pay, maternity, paternity and shared parental pay
  • Workplace pensions: assessing staff, enrolling them, sending contributions, and re-enrolment every three years
  • Year-end: P60s for staff by 31 May, P11D benefit forms by 6 July where needed
  • Directors' payroll, including salary set at a tax-efficient level agreed with you

Moving payroll part way through the year

You can move payroll to us at any point in the tax year. We need your PAYE reference, your Accounts Office reference, and the year-to-date figures for each employee from your current provider. We check those figures, run a parallel month if you want one, and take over from the next pay run. Your staff should notice nothing except the new payslip layout.

Who this is for, and who it isn't

This suits you if you pay between 2 and 50 people and you are tired of being the person who chases timesheets and fixes tax codes.

If you pay one director a fixed salary and nothing else changes, payroll software may be enough on its own. We will say so.

How the fee works

A fixed monthly fee based on how many people you pay and how often. Pension administration is included. Year-end forms are included. Anything unusual, such as a large redundancy round, is quoted before we start it.

Payroll questions

How much does outsourced payroll cost for a small business?

It depends mainly on how many people you pay and how often you run payroll. We agree a fixed monthly fee in writing after a 20-minute review, so you know the cost before you start.

What is RTI?

Real Time Information is how employers report pay, tax and National Insurance to HMRC. A report must be sent on or before each payday. We send it for you.

Do you pay my staff, or do I?

You do, from your own bank account. We prepare the payment file and tell you the totals. Your money never passes through us.

What if I send changes after the cut-off?

Tell us anyway. We will do our best to include them. If it is too late, we will add them to the next run and tell you exactly what that means for the employee.

Do you handle workplace pensions?

Yes. We assess each employee, enrol those who qualify, send contributions to your pension provider and handle re-enrolment every three years.

VAT, year-end accounts and corporation tax, with no last-minute panic

HMRC deadlines do not move. So we plan backwards from them. You will know every date that applies to your business, what we need from you, and when, usually weeks before it matters.

What we file for you

We prepare and submit VAT returns through Making Tax Digital software, prepare your year-end accounts, file them with Companies House, and prepare and file your company tax return (CT600) with HMRC. We also tell you how much corporation tax to pay and by when. You review and approve everything before it is sent.

The deadlines that apply to most UK limited companies

WhatDeadlineWhat we need from you
VAT return and payment1 month and 7 days after the end of each VAT periodSales and purchase records for the period. If we do your books, nothing extra
Annual accounts to Companies House9 months after your company's year endAnswers to our year-end questions and your approval
Corporation tax payment9 months and 1 day after your year endApproval of the tax figure. You pay HMRC directly
Company tax return (CT600) to HMRC12 months after your year endApproval of the return
Confirmation statementOnce a year, within 14 days of your review dateConfirmation that company details have not changed

Note the order. Corporation tax is paid three months before the return is due. Many owners find that out the hard way.

How we stay ahead of the dates

  1. On day one we put every deadline for your company into one calendar, and you get a copy.
  2. Each month your "Done and due" report shows the next two deadlines and anything we need from you.
  3. Before each VAT return we send the draft figures for approval, with any unusual items explained in a sentence.
  4. At year-end we send one list of questions, not a drip of emails, and a draft tax figure early enough for you to plan the payment.

Tax planning

We file on time, reduce errors and plan legally to lower what you owe. That means using the reliefs and allowances your business is entitled to, timing big purchases sensibly, and setting directors' pay at a tax-efficient level agreed with you. Planning happens before the year end, not after it.

Directors' own tax is covered too: see Self Assessment tax returns.

Audit support

If your company needs an audit, or HMRC opens an enquiry, we prepare the records, answer the questions and support you through it. Statutory audits themselves are carried out by a registered audit firm, and we work alongside them.

Making Tax Digital, in plain terms

Every VAT-registered business must keep digital records and submit VAT returns through compatible software. If you still use spreadsheets, we can connect them through approved bridging software, or move you to Xero, QuickBooks, Sage or FreeAgent. Sole traders and landlords now face a similar rule for income tax, phased in by income level from April 2026.

Who this is for

UK limited companies that are VAT registered, or close to the registration threshold, and want returns and year-end handled by the same team that sees their books each month. If you want year-end only and do your own books, we can do that too. Tell us on the call.

VAT and tax questions

When is my VAT return due?

One calendar month and seven days after the end of your VAT period. For a quarter ending 30 November, both the return and the payment are due by 7 January.

When do I have to register for VAT?

When your VAT-taxable turnover goes over the registration threshold in any rolling 12 months, or you expect it to in the next 30 days. We check your turnover each month, so you hear about it early.

Can you take over my VAT returns mid-year?

Yes. We start from your next VAT period and check the last return filed before we submit anything.

Do you deal with HMRC for me?

Yes, once you authorise us as your agent. HMRC letters and calls about your VAT or corporation tax come to us, and we tell you what they mean.

I'm a sole trader. Does Making Tax Digital for Income Tax affect me?

It might. It started in April 2026 for sole traders and landlords above an income threshold, and the threshold falls in later years. Bring your figures to a review and we will tell you if and when it applies.

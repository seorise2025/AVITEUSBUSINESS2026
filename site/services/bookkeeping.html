What outsourced bookkeeping means with Aviteus

Outsourced bookkeeping means a qualified team outside your business keeps your accounting records up to date for you. With Aviteus, that happens every month, in your own software, to a written scope. You keep full access to your books at all times. We do the posting, matching and checking. You send us what only you have: receipts, a few answers, and approval of anything unusual.

What lands in your inbox each month

  • Reconciled bank and card accounts. Every line matched to a bill, a sale or a receipt, to the last day of the month.
  • Bills and sales posted. Supplier bills, sales invoices and credit notes recorded and coded correctly for VAT.
  • A list of what is missing. Short and specific: "3 card payments need receipts", not "please send paperwork".
  • Your monthly management pack. Profit and loss for the month and year to date, balance sheet, cash position, and one page of notes on what moved and why.
  • Your "Done and due" report. What we finished, what is due next, and what we need from you, with dates.

Books three months behind? Start with a catch-up.

Most new clients are behind when they call. That is normal. We bring the records up to date first, as a separate, fixed-price piece of work, then move you onto the monthly routine. You will know the catch-up fee and how long it will take before we start.

Your software, or we will set it up

We work in Xero, QuickBooks, Sage and FreeAgent. If your records live in spreadsheets or a shoebox, we will set up the software, connect your bank, and show you the two or three things you need to do each month. Nothing more.

Who this is for, and who it isn't

This suits you if you run a UK business with regular sales, supplier bills and a few staff, and you want to see your numbers every month without doing the posting yourself.

It may not suit you if you have fewer than 20 transactions a month and no staff. Good software and a quarterly check might be enough. We will tell you that on the call.

How the fee works

One fixed monthly fee, agreed in writing before any work starts. It is based on how many transactions you have, how many bank and card accounts we reconcile, and whether you need management accounts. Catch-up work and software set-up are quoted separately, so you can see each cost on its own.

Bookkeeping questions

How much does bookkeeping cost for a small business?

It depends on volume, not on a price list. A business with 80 transactions a month and one bank account costs less than one with 600 transactions and four accounts. We fix the monthly fee in writing after a 20-minute review, so you know the figure before you commit.

What is the difference between bookkeeping and management accounts?

Bookkeeping is recording and matching every transaction. Management accounts are the short report built from those records: profit, cash and what changed. Good management accounts need up-to-date books first.

Will I still have access to my own books?

Yes, always. The software stays in your name. We work inside it as your adviser, and you can remove our access at any time.

Do you need my paperwork in a particular format?

No. Photos of receipts, forwarded emails and PDFs all work. We will show you the quickest route for your business.

Can you take over from my current bookkeeper part way through the year?

Yes. We pick up from the last reconciled month and check the opening balances before posting anything new.

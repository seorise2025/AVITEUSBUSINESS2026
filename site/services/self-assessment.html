Self Assessment tax returns, filed before 31 January

Your personal tax return, prepared from your figures and filed on time. You see the tax figure early, so the 31 January payment is never a surprise.

Who needs a Self Assessment tax return

You usually need to file a Self Assessment tax return if you are self-employed, earn rental income, or have income that tax has not already been taken from, such as dividends above your allowance. HMRC can also ask you to file. Check GOV.UK, or ask us on a review call.

What is included

  • Registering for Self Assessment, if you are new to it.
  • Gathering your income: salary, dividends, rental income and self-employment.
  • Working out your tax, and any payments on account, with the figure sent to you early.
  • Filing your return online before 31 January, after you approve it.
  • Reminders for the 31 January and 31 July payments.

The Self Assessment dates

WhatDeadline
Register for Self Assessment, if you are new to it5 October after the tax year ends
Paper tax return31 October after the tax year ends
Online tax return and tax due31 January after the tax year ends
First payment on account, if one applies31 January
Second payment on account, if one applies31 July

Payments on account are advance payments towards next year's bill. They often catch people out in their second year, when the first return and the first advance payment fall due on the same day.

Who this is for, and who it isn't

This suits company directors who take dividends, landlords, and self-employed people who want their return done properly and early, not in the last week of January.

If your only income is a salary taxed through PAYE, you may not need to file at all. We will tell you on the call.

How the fee works

A fixed fee for each tax return, based on how many income sources you have. Agreed in writing before any work starts.

Self Assessment questions

When is the Self Assessment deadline?

31 January after the end of the tax year, for online returns and for paying the tax. Paper returns are due earlier, on 31 October.

What are payments on account?

Advance payments towards next year's tax bill, due on 31 January and 31 July. Whether they apply depends on your last bill.

What happens if I file late?

HMRC charges a £100 penalty straight away for a late return, with more if it stays late. Interest is also charged on tax paid late.

Does Making Tax Digital for Income Tax affect me?

It might, if you are self-employed or a landlord above an income threshold. Bring your figures to a review and we will tell you if and when it applies.

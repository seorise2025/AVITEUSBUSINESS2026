Who we are

Aviteus Business Solutions Ltd is the data controller for personal data collected through this website. Company number 16694378, registered in England and Wales. Registered office: 296 Kenton Road, Harrow HA3 8DD.

Questions about your data: email info@aviteusbusiness.co.uk or call 020 4628 8209.

What we collect

  • When you send an enquiry: your full name, email, company or website (if you give it), the service you ask about, your message, how you want us to reply, and your phone or WhatsApp number if you choose a call or WhatsApp.
  • About the visit that led to your enquiry: the page you sent it from, the website that sent you here, and any campaign tags in the link you clicked.
  • If you tick the email box: your email address, to send occasional emails about UK tax deadlines and changes.
  • If you accept analytics cookies: how pages are used, through Google Analytics. See our cookie details.
  • If you message us on WhatsApp: your number and the messages you send.

The deadline finder works in your browser. Nothing you choose in it is saved or sent to us.

Why we use it, and our lawful basis

  • To reply to your enquiry and prepare your review. To confirm: lawful basis, for example steps taken at your request before a contract, or legitimate interests
  • To send occasional emails, only if you tick the box. Lawful basis: your consent. You can withdraw it at any time.
  • To see which pages help people, only if you accept analytics cookies. Lawful basis: your consent.

To confirm: the final list of processors and the agreements with each. The strategy pack names: Zoho CRM, the email service that sends enquiry notifications, SEO Rise (website support and lead notifications), Google Analytics, Cloudflare Turnstile spam checks, and WhatsApp (Meta) for chats.

We do not sell your data.

Your rights

You can ask to see the personal data we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it, to limit how we use it, to object to how we use it, or to receive a copy in a common format.

UK limited company deadlines: the dates every director should know

In short

Most dates follow from your company's year end. Accounts go to Companies House 9 months after it. Corporation tax is paid 9 months and 1 day after it. The company tax return (CT600) is due 12 months after it. VAT is due 1 month and 7 days after each VAT period, and PAYE by the 22nd of each month.

The main dates, with a 31 March year end

WhatRuleYear end 31 March 2026
Annual accounts to Companies House9 months after year end31 December 2026
Corporation tax payment9 months and 1 day after year end1 January 2027
Company tax return (CT600)12 months after year end31 March 2027

Notice the order. You pay corporation tax three months before the return is due. That catches many owners out, because the bill arrives before the paperwork.

Your first accounts are different

A new company's first accounts are due 21 months after the date it was set up. If you are setting one up now, see company formation and compliance. After that, the 9-month rule applies. If your company is new, check this date first, as it is easy to miss.

Dates that repeat through the year

WhatWhen
VAT return and payment1 month and 7 days after each VAT period ends
PAYE and National Insurance22nd of each month if you pay electronically
Payroll report to HMRC (RTI)On or before each payday
Confirmation statementAt least once a year, within 14 days of your review date

More on each: VAT returns and year-end and running payroll.

Yearly payroll dates

  • 31 May: give every employee their P60 for the tax year that ended on 5 April.
  • 6 July: send P11D and P11D(b) forms for benefits in kind, where you have any.

Directors' own tax

  • 31 January: Self Assessment return and any tax due for the tax year that ended the previous April, plus a first payment on account if one applies.
  • 31 July: second payment on account, if one applies.

We prepare and file directors' returns: see Self Assessment tax returns.

What happens if you miss a date

  • Accounts filed late at Companies House: an automatic penalty that grows with time, from £150 for up to a month late to £1,500 for over six months. It doubles if you are late two years running.
  • CT600 filed late: a £100 penalty, and another £100 if it is three months late. More follows after that.
  • Corporation tax paid late: HMRC charges interest from the day after the due date.
  • VAT returns filed late: each late return adds a penalty point. Reach the limit and a penalty follows.

If a date falls on a weekend or bank holiday, make sure payment reaches HMRC by the working day before.

Questions

When is corporation tax due for a small company?

9 months and 1 day after the end of the accounting period. With a 31 March year end, that is 1 January.

When are company accounts due at Companies House?

9 months after the year end for a private limited company, after its first accounts. First accounts are due 21 months after the company was set up.

Is the corporation tax return due at the same time as the payment?

No. The return (CT600) is due 12 months after the year end, three months after the payment.

VAT returns and Making Tax Digital: a plain guide for small businesses

In short

A VAT return and its payment are due 1 calendar month and 7 days after the end of each VAT period. Every VAT-registered business must keep digital records and file through software that works with Making Tax Digital. Keep your VAT records for at least 6 years.

When your VAT return is due

Most businesses file every three months. The return and the payment are both due 1 calendar month and 7 days after the period ends.

Quarter endsReturn and payment due
31 March7 May
30 June7 August
30 September7 November
31 December7 February

If you pay by Direct Debit, HMRC collects the payment a few working days after the due date.

What Making Tax Digital means for VAT

Every VAT-registered business must:

  • Keep its VAT records digitally.
  • Submit returns through software that works with Making Tax Digital.
  • Keep digital links between its records and the return. Copying figures by hand between systems is not allowed.

If you still use spreadsheets, bridging software can link them to HMRC. Most businesses find it simpler to move to Xero, QuickBooks, Sage or FreeAgent.

When you must register for VAT

You must register when your VAT-taxable turnover goes over the registration threshold in any rolling 12 months, or when you expect it to in the next 30 days alone. Check the current threshold on GOV.UK, as it can change. Watching your turnover each month is the easiest way to avoid registering late. The free VAT registration checker works out your rolling total.

Records to keep

  • Sales and purchase invoices.
  • Records of VAT you charged and VAT you paid.
  • Any adjustments, such as credit notes.

Keep them for at least 6 years.

What happens if you file or pay late

  • Late returns: each late return adds a penalty point. Once you reach the limit for your filing frequency, a penalty follows, and so does every late return after it.
  • Late payments: penalties and interest start soon after the due date and grow the longer it is unpaid.

Filing a few days early every quarter is the simplest way to avoid both.

Questions

When is my VAT return due?

One calendar month and seven days after the end of your VAT period. For a quarter ending 31 March, it is 7 May.

Do I have to use Making Tax Digital software?

Yes, if you are VAT registered. Returns must go through compatible software, with digital links between your records and the return.

How long do I keep VAT records?

At least 6 years.

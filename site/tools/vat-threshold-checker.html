VAT registration questions

When do I have to register for VAT?

When your VAT-taxable turnover for the last 12 months goes over the registration threshold (£90,000 in this tool), or when you expect to go over it in the next 30 days alone.

How long do I have to register?

If your rolling 12-month total goes over, register within 30 days of the end of the month you went over. If you expect to go over in the next 30 days alone, register by the end of that 30-day period.

What counts as VAT-taxable turnover?

The total value of everything you sell that is not exempt from VAT. Exempt sales, such as some health, education and insurance services, do not count.

Can I register before I reach the threshold?

Yes. Some businesses register voluntarily, for example to reclaim VAT on costs. Whether it suits you depends on who your customers are. We go through this on a review call.

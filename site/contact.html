    What happens in the 20 minutes

    1. We listen. You tell us what is going on and what is bothering you most.
    2. We look. If you can, share your software screen or last VAT return. If not, we work from what you tell us.
    3. We tell you three things: what is behind, what is due next, and whether Aviteus is the right fit.

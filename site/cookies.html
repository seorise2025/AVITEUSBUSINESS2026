Before you choose

Nothing is set for analytics until you choose. Your choice itself is saved in your browser so we do not ask again on every page.

av_consentRemembers whether you accepted or rejected analytics. Saved in your browser's local storage until you clear it.

If you accept analytics

_gaGoogle Analytics. Tells visits apart. Expires after 2 years.
_ga_ followed by an IDGoogle Analytics. Keeps track of a visit. Expires after 2 years.
av_firstThe first page you visited, so an enquiry shows which page helped. Saved in your browser for this visit only.

About the founder and CEO

Meet Vikas Pandey

Handing over your books, payroll or client work takes trust. I don’t expect you to hand over everything on day one. I’d rather start with one clearly defined piece of work and give you the chance to see how we deliver.

I lead Aviteus Business Solutions from Harrow, London. We help UK businesses with bookkeeping, payroll, VAT, year-end accounts and tax. We also give accountancy practices extra capacity for recurring work such as bookkeeping and reconciliations, so their teams have more time to advise clients.

I also lead Aviteus Healthcare, a separate UK healthcare staffing business established in 2021. Working with care providers has shaped how I lead: get the details right, raise issues early and follow through on what you’ve agreed. Those are the same standards I expect at Aviteus Business Solutions.

Before we start, we’ll agree on what we’ll handle, what it will cost and when you can expect it back. You can then judge us on the work itself and how we communicate along the way. If it works for your business, we can take on more. If you only need help with that one task, that’s fine too.

Why Aviteus exists

Vikas Pandey started Aviteus Healthcare to supply care and nursing staff across the UK. Running that business taught him a simple lesson about money: when payroll is late, real people do not get paid, and when the books are behind, the owner makes decisions blind.

Aviteus Business Solutions was set up in Harrow in September 2025 to give other owner-managed businesses what he needed himself. Books that are current. Payroll that runs on the same day every month. Deadlines known well in advance. And one person to ask.

Yes, we're a new firm

We do not have a wall of reviews yet, and we will not invent one. Here is what being new means in practice:

  • Your work gets senior attention, not a junior working from a checklist.
  • Each client matters to us, so we will work hard to keep you.
  • We set up every process recently, around today's software and Making Tax Digital, not around habits from 1998.

How we work with you

  • Everything in writing first. A scope that lists what we do, what you do, and the fixed fee, before any work starts.
  • One named manager. You will not be passed around. If your manager is away, you will be told who is covering.
  • A monthly report you can read in two minutes. What we finished, what is due next, what we need from you.
  • Your software, your data. Your accounting software stays in your name, and you can remove our access at any time.

Team and credentials

Our accountants hold ICAEW, ACCA and CIMA qualifications. We work across Xero, Sage, QuickBooks and FreeAgent, and we support migrations and training.

We learn your goals, tidy the data, set the right processes and keep you ahead of HMRC dates. You get simple dashboards, monthly insights and early notes on risks and opportunities.

Common questions about our services

What services does Aviteus Business Solutions provide?

Bookkeeping and management accounts, payroll and pensions, VAT returns, year-end accounts and corporation tax, tax planning, Self Assessment, business advisory, Virtual CFO, audit support, company formation and accounting software set-up. We also have specialist knowledge of healthcare, construction and e-commerce.

Do you support Making Tax Digital?

Yes. We use cloud accounting software such as Xero, QuickBooks and Sage, so your business meets HMRC's digital rules for VAT and other tax submissions.

How quickly do you respond to queries?

Each client has a dedicated account manager. We respond within one business day, and urgent matters come first.

What makes Aviteus different from other accountants?

A qualified team (ICAEW, ACCA and CIMA), modern cloud tools and fixed fees agreed in writing. We give advice before problems arrive, not just compliance after them, and we build long-term working relationships around your goals.

Can you help with starting and growing a business?

Yes. We support businesses at every stage: company formation and set-up, growth planning, business valuation and fundraising support, cash flow management and strategic financial planning.

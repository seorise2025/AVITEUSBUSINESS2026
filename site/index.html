From first call to first report in four steps

  1. Review. A free 20-minute call. We look at what is behind, what is due, and whether we are the right fit. Sometimes we are not, and we will say so.
  2. Scope. We send a written scope: what we do, what you do, when, and the fixed monthly fee. Nothing starts until you agree it.

Questions owners ask first

How much will it cost?

A fixed monthly fee, agreed in writing before we start. It depends on how many transactions you have, how many people you pay, and whether you are VAT registered. You will have the figure after your review.

Can I keep my current accountant for the year-end?

Yes. Some owners use us for the monthly work and keep their current firm for the annual accounts. The scope will say who does what.

Which software do you work in?

Xero, QuickBooks, Sage and FreeAgent. If you are on spreadsheets, we can set you up and move your records across.

Do I need to be near Harrow?

No. We work with businesses across the UK by video, phone and email. Our office in Harrow is there if you would rather meet in person.

How does switching work?

We write to your current accountant for professional clearance, collect your records, and get authority to talk to HMRC for you. We time it around your next payroll and VAT dates so nothing is missed.

Bring one problem. Leave with a plan.

Twenty minutes is enough to tell you what is behind, what is due next, and whether we can help. If we can't, we'll tell you who might.
